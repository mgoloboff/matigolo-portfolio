## Stack

## Users

Hiring managers, design directors, startup founders, and project leads evaluating whether Matias Goloboff is the right product designer for their team or project. They arrive with a role or scope in mind and need to quickly assess design thinking, craft, and domain fit — not just a gallery of screens.

## Product Purpose

A portfolio that makes Matias's work legible, his thinking tangible, and his fit clear for decision-makers. Success means a visitor leaves convinced they understand how Matias thinks and works — and wants to reach out.

## Positioning

Specialist in simplifying complex B2B data products. 5 years turning multi-stakeholder analytics and dashboard systems into experiences that feel obvious in use. Psychology & HCI background plus a coding and robotics foundation means he works at the intersection of human cognition, information architecture, and technical feasibility — a combination neighboring generalist designers cannot truthfully claim.

## Operating Context

Visitors are typically reviewing candidates or freelancers. They scan a homepage for signal, click into one or two case studies, and form a judgment in minutes. Mobile review is common; some will share a link with a hiring committee. Case study depth matters — they are looking for evidence of process and problem-solving, not just outcomes.

## Product Principles

1. **Thinking over output.** Show how the problem was understood and the tradeoffs made — not just the final screen.
2. **Earned complexity.** The work lives in complex B2B systems; case studies should reflect that depth without overwhelming the visitor.
3. **Legibility at speed.** Decision-makers scan. Every page must yield its most important signal in the first ten seconds.
4. **Human voice, no performance.** Copy reads as Matias actually talks — curious, direct, grounded — not as a design industry persona.
5. **Restraint as craft.** The portfolio's own design is evidence of taste; it should look like it was made by someone who knows when to stop.
